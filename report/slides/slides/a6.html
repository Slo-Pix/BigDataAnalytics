Sci-Fi and Horror are pirated far beyond their popularity

Model: log(1 + torrent versions) ~ log(IMDb votes) + release year, on all 8,024 IMDb films of 2018–2022 with ≥1,000 votes — including the 2,507 never pirated.

A genre's average residual = how many more (or fewer) versions it gets than an equally popular film of the same year.

Sci-Fi +59%, Horror +56% · Romance −18%, Comedy −12%

12 · R² 0.19; bars show 95% confidence intervals; genre is partly confounded with language

Including films with no torrent matters: otherwise we would only study films that were already pirated. Popularity explains about a fifth of the variation; each extra 1 percent of IMDb votes brings about 0.36 percent more torrent versions. Genre and language are mixed up — English-language genre films are more likely to be on an English-language site.
