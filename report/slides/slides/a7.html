Our first version of this chart was wrong, and that taught us something. Taking the maximum over trackers made the curve dip whenever a tracker went down, and torrents chosen because they were alive at noon showed regression to the mean. The fix: one tracker that never went down, a fixed panel of torrents alive most of the day, and each torrent normalised to its own average. One day cannot show whether the game pattern repeats.
