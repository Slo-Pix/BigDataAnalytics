How it ran: tier 1 scraped 16,555 known-alive torrents about every 2 minutes; tier 2 scraped 110,036 more alive torrents found by a full catalog scan, every 11 to 20 minutes. Each micro-batch id is written to a ledger table in the same transaction as its data, so a replay after a crash is skipped — exactly-once; we tested it by deleting the checkpoint and replaying. A scheduled stop at 21:00 stopped the scrapers, let Spark drain Kafka and stopped the stream. It survived a reboot on battery loss, two tracker outages and a slow phone hotspot.
