What we learned

Most of the work was finding where the data lies

Time zones

PySpark shifted stream times 5 h 30 min into local time — fixed by forcing UTC

Broken headers

Steam CSV had 39 header names for 40 columns — every column after Price was shifted

Survivorship

Old torrents looked healthier because unpopular ones were deleted — analyse per title

Selection effects

Torrents picked for being alive at noon faded by 13:30 — use a fixed panel

Limits: one site (closed 2023), one day of live data, trackers see part of each swarm, associations not causes.
