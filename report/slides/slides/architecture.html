Batch layer recomputes everything from immutable raw data. Speed layer: scrapers speak the BitTorrent UDP scrape protocol (BEP 15) to five public trackers and publish every observation to Kafka; Spark Structured Streaming reads Kafka in one-minute micro-batches, appends to Parquet and upserts summaries to PostgreSQL. Grafana reads PostgreSQL. Changes from the proposal: Hive became Spark SQL, Airflow became shell runners plus cron, HDFS was defined but not needed on one machine.
